# Alya

Imported from the user’s Original AI Avatar package. Original geometry, textures, blazer, hair, rig and facial controls are preserved byte for byte inside lossless gzip. The poster is its actual Blender render. No stock avatar model was substituted.

Browser rendering: Three.js (MIT). English text-to-mouth estimates: TalkingHead LipsyncEn (MIT). Speech: the server-generated Kokoro Heart English voice (Apache-2.0). The same voice is used for all visitors. Mouth movement follows audio playback with approximate phoneme timing.
